## Provisional Commercial Estimates

These are reasoned planning estimates for discussion, not verified selling
prices. Public Nigerian terminal price evidence is sparse. Validate with three
written competitor/implementation quotes and prospective-customer interviews.

### Replacement Development

Budget NGN 8m-20m to commission a broadly comparable custom platform with its
documented role, stage, finance, report, reversal, AI and regression depth,
handover and normal implementation work. The premium over a small clearing
package is a scope/risk inference, not a known completed sales transaction.
Hardware, specialist terminal automation, large migrations and 24/7 SLA delivery
would need additional quotation. This is not what the existing codebase or
company is necessarily worth to a buyer. No exclusive-IP buyout value assigned.

### Proposed Subscription Ranges

Per customer company, not per staff member. Define users/sites/storage/support
and AI quotas in any actual offer; the following are not implemented plans.

| Customer scope | Monthly NGN | Annual NGN at 12 monthly payments |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Small clearing business, limited package | 75,000-150,000 | 900,000-1,800,000 |
| Established operator, broader workflows and finance | 150,000-350,000 | 1,800,000-4,200,000 |
| More demanding multi-branch/terminal workflow contract | 350,000-750,000 | 4,200,000-9,000,000 |

The upper tier assumes scoped implementation/support needs; it is not a claim
the app already replaces a full industrial TOS. Do not offer unlimited storage,
AI, customization or round-the-clock support without cost modelling.

Suggested initial offer to validate: NGN 1m one-time setup/migration/training;
NGN 200,000/month or NGN 2m prepaid/year for a bounded mid-sized customer.
Annual package first year: NGN 3m including setup; renewal: NGN 2m, excluding
taxes, unusual usage and separately agreed development. A 10-customer example at
NGN 2m/year is NGN 20m recurring gross revenue, not profit or guaranteed demand.

### Running Cost of One Modest Installation

Planning assumption: 10-30 staff, 1-3 branches, roughly 100-300 jobs/month,
ordinary documents and capped light AI/messaging, without terminal hardware or
24/7 support. These are not measurements of the user's current bill.

- Cloud application/database/storage/backups plus modest API/messaging allowance:
  NGN 50,000-150,000/month, or NGN 600,000-1,800,000/year.
- Budgeted human maintenance/support: NGN 100,000-300,000/month, or
  NGN 1,200,000-3,600,000/year; contract scope determines the actual fee.
- Combined planning envelope: NGN 150,000-450,000/month, or
  NGN 1,800,000-5,400,000/year, excluding major development, hardware, taxes and
  on-site travel. Annualized amounts simply multiply monthly estimates by 12.

Railway currently publishes Pro at USD 20/month including USD 20 usage; excess
usage raises the bill. It is not a flat cap for the entire app, databases,
documents, AI, email and professional support. No current exchange rate was
assumed and no live billing account was accessed. Validate usage from actual
invoices before accepting a fixed-price support contract. Shared product
maintenance may spread across customers, but separate-deployment/cloud costs
and customer support still need allocation; the suggested selling price is not
a guaranteed profitable price at every operating-cost point above.
